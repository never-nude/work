Install

Four steps.

Open the disk image

Double-click ThreadKeep-1.0.dmg, then drag ThreadKeep into your Applications folder.

Launch it

Open ThreadKeep from Applications. It's signed and notarized, so it opens normally. If macOS ever hesitates, right-click the app and choose Open once.

Allow ThreadKeep to read Messages

macOS asks your permission first. In System Settings → Privacy & Security, turn on the switch for ThreadKeep, then reopen the app. Access is read-only and stays on your Mac.

Import your messages

Click Import Messages to build your library — everything, or just the conversations you choose.
