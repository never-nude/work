01

Atrium

Five thousand years of sculpture, up close.

A digital museum of 363 sculptures you can rotate, relight, and study in 3D — from a 3,000-year-old Assyrian relief to Rodin. I built the pipeline that pulls open museum records, generates the 3D, and shows only the facts that can be confirmed. Breadth, taste, and a real data backend behind a gallery-quiet interface.

Live 3D / WebGL Astro Data pipeline

02

Ad Arma — The War Council

Draft your orders. Bribe the dice. Spring the trap.

A classical-era tactics game — Rome against Carthage on a hex battlefield. Draft a hidden "war council" of orders, deploy in secret, and read your opponent like a poker hand while dice-driven combat plays out. Three AI commanders with distinct temperaments, multiple battlefields, and online head-to-head. An original ruleset, engineered so cause and effect are always visible on the board.

WPCNA

Civic software people actually use.

The neighborhood hub for the White Plains Council of Neighborhood Associations — an interactive neighborhood map, aggregated public events, meeting agendas, and community postings. Real software in production, used by residents and volunteers who never think about the code underneath. Built for people, not engineers.

Treefort

A private clubhouse network: forts you knock to enter, each with a wall, a paint room, and a gif machine. No accounts, no feeds, no analytics — the whole thing runs on a single Cloudflare Worker with zero dependencies. And yes, the management is a dog.

ThreadKeep

The conversations worth keeping, preserved properly.

A native macOS app for archiving iMessage threads — built around a data source Apple doesn't make easy. Local-first, private by default, and designed with the restraint a native Mac app deserves. A different discipline from the web work: SwiftUI, system frameworks, real platform craft.

06

Worldbook

A layered world map, on a spinning globe.

An interactive 3D globe reference work: sourced data on every country — religion, politics, economy, health, debt, trade, migration — as switchable map layers, with a time scrubber to watch the world change. Drag to spin, click a country to drill in. Part atlas, part data instrument.

AI-native execution

AI-assisted development is my force multiplier; product judgment, UX, and systems design are the parts that don't come for free. The result is a small number of complete, shipped things.
